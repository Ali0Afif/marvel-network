Welcome to the Marvel Network Museum — an interactive exploration of 303 Marvel superheroes connected through their Wikipedia pages. WHAT ARE WE EXPLORING?

We are not mapping the Marvel universe exactly as it exists in the comics. We are mapping how the Marvel universe is represented through Wikipedia links. Every directed edge is an editorial act: one character's Wikipedia page chose to mention another.

Every connection tells us something about how these characters are organised, referenced and connected — not by their stories, but by how the internet writes about them.
