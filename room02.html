Who gets talked about — and who does the talking?

106 vs 9 Spider-Man's in vs out degree. 106 heroes reference him. He references 9 back. The gap is not a quirk — it is the whole phenomenon.

An in-link is conferred: another article's editors decided this character was worth mentioning. An out-link is authored: someone sat down and wrote it into this page. One has no ceiling. The other has a budget. Two different generating mechanisms — two different distributions.

THE BIG QUESTION

IS BEING POPULAR THE SAME AS BEING WELL CONNECTED?

The scatter plot shows they are not. Spider-Man is famous and referenced, but he connects outward to very few. Betsy Braddock links to 28 other characters — more than most landmarks — but only 7 link back to her. Popularity is conferred. Connectivity is authored. Different characters, different roles in the same network.

Spider-Man doesn't just have high centrality — he has become a reference point. Editors invoke him as context for characters who never met him. He is the network's "you are here" sign.
