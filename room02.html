EXHIBIT 03  ·  TWO KINDS OF POWER
106 vs 9 SPIDER-MAN'S IN-DEGREE vs OUT-DEGREE

106 pages link to Spider-Man. He links back to only 9. In-links are conferred by others' editorial choices — no ceiling. Out-links are authored decisions — a finite budget. Popularity is passive. Connection is active. They are not the same thing.

IN vs OUT DEGREE · Popularity ≠ Connectivity · Two distinct generating mechanisms
