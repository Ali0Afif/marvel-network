EXHIBIT 01  ·  THE DISCOVERY

ON AVERAGE, YOUR CONNECTIONS ARE MORE CONNECTED THAN YOU.

This is the friendship paradox — a mathematical property of almost every real-world network. It sounds impossible. But it holds for the vast majority of Marvel heroes. Not because the universe is unfair. Because of the way networks are built.

THE FRIENDSHIP PARADOX · Feld, 1991 · Holds for 83%+ of Marvel heroes
EXHIBIT 02  ·  THE MECHANISM

SPIDER-MAN IS EVERYONE'S NEIGHBOUR.

The paradox exists because hubs appear in many heroes' connection lists. When Spider-Man (106 in-links) is your neighbor, he pulls your average up — dramatically. Not bad luck. The structure of the network asserting itself. The more skewed the degree distribution, the stronger the paradox.

HUB EFFECT · Skewed degree distribution · Mean neighbor degree > your degree

EXHIBIT 03  ·  THE EXCEPTION
106 SPIDER-MAN · THE PARADOX PERSONIFIED

With 106 incoming links, Spider-Man doesn't suffer the friendship paradox — he causes it for everyone else. His neighbors cannot pull his average above him. He IS the hub. The paradox breaks down exactly at the centre of the network. Find a hero with more popular friends than Spider-Man. You can't.
